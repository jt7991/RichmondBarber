## Begin with the look that feels like you

Your wedding haircut should feel like a considered version of your own style. We want you to look forward to the photographs and feel comfortable wearing the result through the ceremony and celebration.

Start by choosing what you want to keep. You might love your usual length and want the outline refreshed, or you might want more movement on top and a different beard shape. Bring a favorite photo of your own haircut along with any inspiration, and tell us what makes each one appealing.

We recommend exploring a significant change several weeks before the wedding. That gives you time to enjoy the haircut in everyday life, practice the styling, and decide which details you want repeated for the final visit.

## Use a trial to find your ideal timing

Some people love their hair immediately after a cut; others prefer the softer appearance after several washes. We recommend using a trial to find your own timing instead of choosing a date from a universal rule.

After the trial, take a few photos on different days using your normal styling routine. Notice when the front sits the way you like, how the sides look, and how easily you can recreate the finish. Tell us which version you prefer when planning the final appointment.

For example, if you love your haircut five days after the trial, we can use that preference as a starting point when discussing your wedding schedule. The useful detail is your experience with the actual style, rather than an assumed number of days that suits every haircut.

## Plan backward from your first photographs

Your first important photographs may happen before the ceremony. We recommend considering the rehearsal, getting-ready pictures, and travel as you choose your appointment date.

| Planning stage | What to decide with us |
| --- | --- |
| Several weeks ahead | Your preferred haircut, beard shape, and styling direction |
| After your trial | How many days after a cut you like the result best |
| Booking your final visit | The services and appointment time that fit your plans |
| Wedding week | Your confirmed schedule and familiar styling routine |
| Photo day | Enough time to groom, dress, and feel ready |

We suggest keeping your final request clear and familiar. Bring a photo of the successful trial and tell us what you want repeated. If you would like a small adjustment, identify the area specifically, such as the fringe or the beard's side profile.

## Give your beard its own plan

Your beard and haircut may look their best to you on different schedules. Tell us whether you want to keep a full beard, refine a shorter shape, or choose a clean-shaven finish. We can then discuss the appropriate services for your intended look.

If you are changing from a beard to a shave, we recommend trying that appearance well ahead of the wedding. The same goes for an unfamiliar shave routine: give yourself time to see how you like the finish and how your skin responds.

## Frequently asked questions

### Should I book my wedding haircut for the day before?

We recommend that timing if you already know you love the look so soon after a cut. A trial gives you a more personal answer and helps us plan around your preference.

### When should I schedule my wedding beard trim?

Tell us when you usually like your beard best after a trim and when your photos begin. We can discuss your beard appointment separately from your haircut if that suits the look you want.

### Can I arrange visits for my wedding party?

Call us to discuss the services and appointment availability for your group. We recommend confirming each person's reservation before adding the visits to your shared schedule.
